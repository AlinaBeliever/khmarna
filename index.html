About Me

Hi, I'm Oksana

I'm originally from Ukraine, and I've been capturing genuine emotions and creating beautiful memories for over two years. Photography is more than just a job for me — it is something I genuinely love. I put my whole heart into every session and every image I create.

I'm also a wife and a mom to a little boy, so I understand how important patience, kindness, and a comfortable atmosphere are during a photo session. Alongside photography, I work as a preschool teacher, which makes me a wonderful choice for children's and family photography. I know how to help kids feel relaxed, happy, and natural in front of the camera.

Before becoming a photographer, I was involved in modeling — which gave me valuable experience with posing. I always guide my clients through poses and help them feel confident and natural throughout the entire session.
